01

The working principle

Let the structure show. Give every element a purpose, and every piece of information a clear place.

Use the browser’s own type. Let proportion, rhythm, and a little blue ink do the work.

Second specimen · For discussion

I. THE WRITTEN WORD

Type carries the hierarchy.

Sans serif establishes structure. Serif explains. Monospace specifies.

II. THE PRINTED PAGE

Order before ornament.

Margins, rules, and alignment give the page its shape. Related things stay close together.

III. THE WORKING TOOL

Make the next action clear.

Links look like links. Controls have labels. States are expressed in words as well as colour.
